# Sources for Chapter 64, section 64.5 (checked 29 September 2026)

Law changes. Every date and duty in section 64.5 comes from the sources below, as they stood on
29 September 2026. Before you rely on any of it, check the official source again: that habit is the
point of the section. This is not legal advice.

How they were checked: by web search on 29 September 2026. The official sites (MeitY, PIB, EUR-Lex)
could not be opened directly from the machine used to write the chapter, so each fact below rests on the
official document's published text as quoted in search results, and on at least one independent legal
summary that agrees with it. A lawyer's review before print is recommended.
