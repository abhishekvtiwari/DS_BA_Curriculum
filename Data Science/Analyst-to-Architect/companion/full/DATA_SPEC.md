# Riverstone Supplies — full sales dataset (2023–2025)

*Companion data for Analyst to Architect. Riverstone Supplies is fictional; every name and number is invented.*

Generated by `companion/generate_riverstone_full.py` (seed **20230101**), which reads `generate_riverstone_2025.py` (seed 20251). Re-running both scripts reproduces every row exactly. Verified by `checks/full_dataset_checks.py` (14 checks, all passing) on PostgreSQL 16.15 and MySQL 8.0.46.

First used in: Chapter 14 (Data Cleaning), then Chapters 16, 18, 20, 21–23, and the Chapter 12 and 13 projects ("the full-size version, with thousands of orders").

| File | Contents |

CSV conventions: UTF-8, comma-separated, header row, dates `YYYY-MM-DD`, timestamps `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`, empty field = NULL. Parquet files carry real date and nullable integer types.

## Loading

**PostgreSQL** (run from this folder):

```
createdb riverstone_full
psql -d riverstone_full -f riverstone_full_setup_postgresql.sql
```

**MySQL** (run from this folder; local file loading must be allowed on the server with `SET GLOBAL local_infile = 1;`):

```
mysql --local-infile=1 -u root -p < riverstone_full_setup_mysql.sql
```

**pandas:** `pd.read_parquet("orders.parquet")` (needs `pyarrow`), or `pd.read_csv("orders.csv", parse_dates=["order_date"])`.

**Excel / Power Query:** the CSVs open directly; `order_items.csv` (209,006 rows) fits in a sheet but belongs in the Data Model (Chapter 11, section 11.8).

## Generation rules

- **Customers.** IDs 1–24 are the named key accounts from the one-year database, unchanged. IDs 25–4979 are generated: segment Retail 55% / Hospitality 30% / Wholesale 15%; cities from 39 Indian cities weighted toward the metros; signup dates 2020–2025 (48% before 2023); buying profile steady, occasional, big (Wholesale), churn, or seasonal; one sales rep assigned by region.
- **Sales coverage.** Employees 1–5 are the original team (Anita Rao, Sales Head; Vikram Singh, Sales Manager; Neha Kulkarni, Rahul Mehta, Farah Khan, sales executives). Employees 6–16 are the regional organization: Arjun Nair (Regional Sales Manager, South), Pooja Desai (RSM West), Sandeep Gill (RSM North), and eight sales executives. There is no separate East manager: **Pooja Desai (RSM West) also covers East (Kolkata)** until a fourth RSM is hired, and the East customers' reps report to the West, South, and North managers. (Coordinator issue 3; the wider two-channel split of key accounts and regional sales is still with the author, and would change which rep is on each regional order, not any revenue figure.)
- **Orders.** Monthly order probability by profile × the one-year database's seasonality (weak June–July, peak October–November) × company growth (2023 80%, 2024 92%, 2025 100%). Steady and big accounts often place two or three orders a month. Order days 1–28. 4% cancelled; orders from 20 December 2025 are Shipped or Pending. About 3% of orders have no sales rep.
- **Lines.** 1–3 products per order from segment-specific product mixes (Wholesale: crates and large boxes with 5–12% discounts; Hospitality: kitchenware with 0–5%; Retail: storage and kitchen, garden chairs in March–May), quantities in multiples of 5 scaled by season.
- **Prices.** `order_items.unit_price` is the list price of the order's year: 2023 = 92% and 2024 = 96% of the current price, rounded to ₹5 (Storage Box 10L: ₹395, ₹415, ₹430). `products.unit_cost` is today's cost and is used for every year (see simplifications).
- **Key accounts.** Customers 1–24 keep exactly their 175 orders and 330 lines of 2025 (order IDs 10001–10175, line IDs 1–330). Those who signed up in 2024 also get 2024 orders; they have no 2023 orders. Their 2025 net revenue is ₹4,335,471, as in Chapters 10–13.
- **IDs.** Generated orders are numbered in date order from 200001; generated lines from 1001. The key accounts' 2025 orders keep their original IDs, so order IDs are **not** in date order across the whole table: sort by `order_date`, not `order_id`.
- **Targets.** Each month's target = the same month's actual revenue in the previous year × that year's growth × a planning error of −6% to +9%, rounded to ₹50,000. (2023 targets use 2023 actuals with the planning error, because 2022 isn't included.)

Unavoidable differences, and how chapters should handle them:

1. **Company totals don't match earlier chapters.** Earlier chapters describe the 24 key accounts. In the full data, "Riverstone's 2025 revenue" is ₹1,146,641,651; the key accounts' share (₹4,335,471) is 0.4%. Chapters using the full data should say "all customers" or "the key accounts" explicitly.
2. **Targets are different tables of numbers.** `sales_targets` in the full data is company-wide; the one-year targets remain in `riverstone_2025`.
3. **The mini database is a separate world** (2026 dates and signup dates that differ from the one-year and full data), as it already was between Chapters 12 and 13. No 2026 data is included in the full dataset.
4. **Invoices and payments** (mini only) aren't in the full dataset; the Finance domain is planned separately (blueprint section 6).

## Simplifications

- Unit costs don't change over time, so older years show lower margins because only prices rose (2023 21.1% → 2025 27.5%). Treat margin trends in this dataset as illustrative.
- No returns, credit notes, or partial deliveries; a cancelled order is cancelled in full.
- Order days never fall after the 28th of a month.
- Leads and lead history cover 2025 only.
