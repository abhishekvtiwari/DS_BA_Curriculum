# The books

*Analyst to Architect* is published as four books, split by part; chapters stay whole. Rebuild them with
`python tools/pdf/books.py book1 book2 book3 book4 map` from `Data Science/Analyst-to-Architect/` (see
`source/BUILD.md`).

| File | Book | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| `Analyst-to-Architect-Book-1-Theory.pdf` | **1. Theory**: the ideas, with no software needed | How to Use This Book; Part 0, First Principles; Part 1, The Map |
| `Analyst-to-Architect-Book-2-Practical.pdf` | **2. Practical**: the analyst's tools, hands on (job-ready at the end of Part 2) | Part 2, The Analyst; Part 3, Advanced Analytics & Analytics Engineering |
| `Analyst-to-Architect-Book-3-Implementation.pdf` | **3. Implementation**: building real systems | Parts 4 to 7 (machine learning, data engineering, production ML and generative AI, architecture and leadership), then the closing chapter |
| `Analyst-to-Architect-Book-4-Be-Interview-Ready.pdf` | **4. Be Interview Ready** | Part 8: how data hiring works, the extra-points method, question banks for each role, take-home assignments and mock interviews |
| `Analyst-to-Architect-Book-Overview.pdf` | **Book Overview** (internal) | Every part and chapter with the skills it covers, the time it needs, what it builds on and its sections, plus a figure of how the parts flow, the reading order, and the routes by role from Chapter 8 |

How the books are laid out:

- **Covers.** Each book has its own.
- **"The whole book".** Every book opens with this map, which lists every part and chapter with its book and
  page.
- **Contents.** Each book then has its own contents, listing every chapter's numbered sections with their
  page numbers.
- **Page numbers.** Books 1 to 3 share one page count, so each starts where the one before ended. Book 4 has
  its own.
- **Parts.** Every part starts with an opening page listing its chapters and their time needed.
- **Chapters.** Every chapter starts on a new page. It opens with a "Chapter at a glance" box: the skills it
  teaches, what to read first, the time needed and the tools. A chapter map follows the box, showing the six
  stages (Start, Learn, Apply, Review, Practise, Next) with page numbers.
- **Footers.** The running footer names the part or chapter.
