# Visual review — Ch21-Descriptive-Statistics-and-Probability.pdf (25 pages)
Verdict: This is the best-set chapter in the batch. The four charts are clean matplotlib graphics at close to readable size (about 6.3–7 pt), with sensible colours and labelled reference lines. Its main visible fault is the section 21.9 `profile()` output: the table's last two columns wrap onto separate lines, which breaks the table the whole section builds to. The rest are the shared patterns: column-0 code continuations, clipped exercise numbers, and cover/contents labels.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 24").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Chart text is converted to outlines (it is not extractable), so it was measured from pixels at 600 dpi. "₹", "≈", "≠" and arrows fall back to DejaVu Serif in body text (pp. 3, 15–16, 19–22, 24–25). No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster. Figures 21.1–21.4 are vector.
- **Figure text size** (ink height at 600 dpi): tick labels about 6.6 pt; in-plot annotations ("median 5.7", "IQR 3.5 d · p95 12.0 d") about 6.3–6.9 pt; panel titles are bold and larger. This is just under the 7 pt target and legible.
- **Text outside margins:** exercise numbers "10."–"21." at x = 48 pt, first digit clipped (pp. 21–22).
- **Blank-area scan:** no half-empty pages. p. 25 is the chapter end.
- **Code-wrap scan:** column-0 continuations on pp. 10, 16 (code) and p. 16 (output).
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets, and all four figures, the tables and the code/output blocks were zoomed at 200–600 dpi.
