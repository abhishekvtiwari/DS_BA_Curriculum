# Visual review — Ch38-Unsupervised-Learning.pdf (33 pages)

Verdict: The figures here are more varied than in the rest of Part IV: a dual-axis line chart, a small-multiples bar panel, a dendrogram and three scatter panels. Two are well made (38.2, 38.4). The chapter's biggest problem is pandas and mlxtend output wrapping back to column 0 inside output blocks. The market-basket rules table (p. 20), the cluster profile (p. 10) and the anomaly list (p. 16) have every row broken across two lines, so the numbers sit under the wrong headings. The dendrogram uses a different (matplotlib) style and a red-next-to-green palette that doesn't match the cluster colours used everywhere else.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 32").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora (Type 3) body; Poppins headings; DejaVu Sans tables and figures; DejaVu Sans Mono code. DejaVu Serif fallback on pp. 18, 21, 26–28 (₹ ×8, √, ✓). No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster. Fig 38.3 (dendrogram) has its text converted to outlines (not extractable). Estimated from the 150 dpi render: tick and axis labels about 7 pt, "cut here for 4 clusters" about 6 pt.
- **Figure text size** (bbox): Fig 38.1 5.9–6.6 pt; Fig 38.2 6.7 pt (sub-labels); Fig 38.4 legend 5.5 pt, panel titles about 6 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** two-digit list numbers at x ≈ 47.7 pt (pp. 24, 26, 27).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 4 0.59 (the "In plain English" page, ~35% short), p. 9 0.75, p. 12 0.74, p. 8/10 0.79–0.82, answers pp. 30–31 about 0.8, p. 33 0.71 (end).
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets. All four figures, all output blocks, the formula boxes and both tables were zoomed at 150 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Output wrapping to column 0** (pp. 5, 10, 16, 20, 28). Output blocks are about 95 monospace characters wide, and wide pandas frames exceed that. This is the chapter's most visible defect. A render-time check for any output line over the block width would catch it.
- **Dual-axis line charts with colour-only series** recur (Fig 38.1 here, Fig 37.1 in Ch37).
- **Formula callouts** (light-blue, dark rule) for hand calculations: support / confidence / lift (p. 18), silhouette. They are consistent, and ÷ / √ / ² render correctly.
- **Callouts:** "Watch out" (peach) on pp. 7, 12, 15, 21; consistent.
- **Folios** "n / 32", offset −1.
