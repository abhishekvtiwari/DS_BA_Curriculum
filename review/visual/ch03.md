# Visual review — Ch03-How-a-Business-Runs-on-Data.pdf (24 pages)
Verdict: Figure-heavy and well structured, with strong tables. The order-to-cash flow (Figures 3.2 and 3.4), which the chapter keeps sending readers back to, is set at 4.7–5.8 pt and cannot be read in print. Figure 3.1 has the same problem. The page flow loses a quarter page to a moved figure, and it splits the Common mistakes table 1 + rest and a lead-in from its table.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio ("n / 23") is PDF page − 1.

- **Fonts:** all embedded. "₹" falls back to DejaVu Serif / Serif-Bold / Serif-Italic on 14 pages (3, 6, 7, 9–12, 16, 17, 19–21, 23, 24). Bold ₹ in tables and totals is the most visible. No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 3.1 5.3 pt; Fig 3.2 4.7–5.6 pt; Fig 3.3 5.5–6.6 pt (value labels 5.5 pt); Fig 3.4 4.7–5.8 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** exercise numbers 10–15 at x < 50 pt (p. 21 and the Stretch list).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 4 25%, p. 19 15%, p. 24 52% (chapter end).
- Every page was viewed at 70 dpi. All four figures, every table and the Meera note were zoomed at 150–200 dpi.
