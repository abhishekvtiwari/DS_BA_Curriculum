# Visual review — Ch55-Building-AI-Applications.pdf (31 pages)

Verdict: The code and prose are cleanly set, and the SQL/Python output blocks read well. The four figures carry the chapter's measured argument but are set at 5.2–5.9 pt. In Figure 55.4 the right-hand annotations are cut off at the frame. Several code-output lines wrap back to column 0, which breaks the retrieval printouts that readers are asked to compare. Pages 10–11 both end about a third empty, and two answer numbers are stranded at page feet.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 30").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded (Poppins headings, Lora 10.4 pt body, DejaVu Sans tables/figures, DejaVu Sans Mono code). "₹" falls back to DejaVu Serif on p. 22 (Lora lacks the glyph). No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster; all figures are vector.
- **Figure text size (pdfplumber):** Fig 55.1 (p. 4) 5.2–5.9 pt, title 6.6; Fig 55.2 (p. 12) 5.5–5.7 pt; Fig 55.3 (p. 14) 5.4–5.7 pt; Fig 55.4 (p. 18) 5.5–5.7 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** only the two-digit exercise numbers at x = 48 pt (pp. 24, 26, 27).
- **Blank-area scan (text ends above 80%):** p. 10 63%, p. 11 66%, p. 15 71%, p. 6 77%, p. 7 78%, p. 13 78%, p. 31 78% (chapter end).
- Every page was viewed on 3×2 contact sheets at 70 dpi. All four figures, the code-output blocks, the tables and the answer pages were zoomed at 150 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)

- **Code/output wraps to column 0** (pp. 7, 9, 11, 19, 28, 29).
- **Page-foot strandings** (lead-in or answer number): pp. 13, 28, 29.
- **Callouts:** Watch out (peach, p. 5) and Simplification note (blue, p. 23) are styled consistently with the rest of the book.
- **Tables:** the "Common mistakes" table splits pp. 21→22 and repeats its header correctly.
- **Folios:** "n / 30", chapter-local; the running footer is correct on every page.
