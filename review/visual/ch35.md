# Visual review — Ch35-The-Math-Under-the-Models.pdf (44 pages)

Verdict: A long, careful chapter. The formula boxes (light blue with a blue left rule) are consistent, and the worked hand calculations are laid out legibly. The six figures are the clearest in this batch, though they are still 5–6 pt. The mathematical notation has real defects:
- the transpose Xᵀ prints as "Xˢ";
- the Bernoulli box runs two formulas together as "P(won) = p P(not won) = 1 − p";
- the binomial and Poisson formulas switch to caret notation (p^k, λ^k × e^(−λ)) right next to formulas typeset with real superscripts (0.2⁶, p⁶(1 − p)²⁴);
- square roots have no vinculum and break across lines.

The cover uses a different status badge ("Approved chapter · v1") and title format from every other chapter in this batch.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 43").

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Maths typography is plain-text Unicode in Lora.** It works for simple arithmetic (× ÷ − ², shown correctly), but it is inconsistent for exponents (Unicode superscripts vs "^"), has no radical overbar, and falls back to DejaVu for ᵀ ⁶ ⁰ ₂ λ ₹. A single maths renderer (KaTeX to SVG/MathML) for the formula boxes would fix V35.1–V35.4 and V35.14 together.
- **Italic variables** (w, b, p, k, n) are italicized in prose and boxes. That is consistent, and good.
- **Figures scaled down:** all six are at 4–6 pt (pp. 8, 17, 18, 23, 28, 31).
- **Colour-only distinctions:** Fig 35.6 segments; Fig 35.3 rates (but these are direct-labelled, so fine).
- **Tables:** clean house style; numeric columns left-aligned throughout.
- **Callouts:** Watch out, Simplification note, Spreadsheet link, Python link, Try it and Interview extra point are all consistent. "Spreadsheet link" and "Python link" are new callout types that don't appear in Chs 30–34.
- **Folios** "n / 43"; the footer is correct.
