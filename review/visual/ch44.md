# Visual review — Ch44-Capstone-An-End-to-End-Data-Science-Project.pdf (17 pages)
Verdict: A short, tidy capstone with no figures. The code, the two small tables and the model memo are consistently styled. The cover subtitle is cut off mid-phrase with "wrap a…", which is the most visible defect. As in Ch27, the model memo's To/From/Re header runs together on one line. Two section openers end their pages with a single lead-in line above a quarter-blank page, and two "How it works" / answer labels are stranded at page feet.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio ("n / 16") is PDF page − 1.
