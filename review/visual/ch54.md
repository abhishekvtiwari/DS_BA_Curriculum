# Visual review — Ch54-Generative-AI-and-LLMs.pdf (32 pages)
Verdict: A strong chapter with good measured figures (54.2, 54.4) and well-structured tables. The model-landscape price table on p. 21 has the "$"-as-maths fault, so three of its four rows are garbled italics, and answer 13 (p. 31) has the same fault. The sampling output on p. 10 wraps so badly that its columns cannot be read. Figures are out of order and below 7 pt, and the cover eyebrow runs into the rule as in Ch53.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 31").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body; DejaVu Sans Mono 7.9 pt code. DejaVu Serif is used as the fallback for "₹" (p. 20). No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 54.1 (p. 5) 5.7–6.7 pt; Fig 54.4 (p. 11) 5.5–6.6 pt; Fig 54.2 (p. 14) 5.7–6.6 pt; Fig 54.3 (p. 17) 5.2–6.6 pt.
- **Figure order (caption scan):** 54.1 p. 5 → 54.4 p. 11 → 54.2 p. 14 → 54.3 p. 17.
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 2 45%; p. 12 about 25% (V54.15).
- **Garbled maths:** found on pp. 21 and 31 by reading at 150 dpi.
- Every page was viewed on contact sheets. All figures, tables and code/output blocks were zoomed at 150–250 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **"$" parsed as maths** (pp. 21, 31). Shared with Ch49. The build needs `$` escaping globally.
- **Figure numbering out of page order** (as in Ch53).
- **Figure text below 7 pt** (pp. 5, 11, 14, 17).
- **Code wraps to column 0** (pp. 8, 10, 12, 15, 22).
- **Two-digit list numbers clipped** (pp. 27, 28).
- **Page-foot strandings:** pp. 12, 15, 25, 28, 31.
- **Folios** "n / 31", correct.
