# Visual review — Ch39-Evaluation-Tuning-Interpretation-and-Honesty.pdf (34 pages)

Verdict: A well-structured chapter. Output blocks fit their width, the model-card table and "Common mistakes" table are clean, and ₹ renders correctly in figures and output. The recurring problems are the book-wide ones: five figures at 5.5–6.9 pt, curve labels printed on top of the curves (Fig 39.2), and page breaks that leave big blanks (p. 10 is 45% empty) or orphan two code lines (p. 20). The calibration chart (Fig 39.3) relies on a red vs teal/blue colour split, with the two well-calibrated models crowded into the bottom-left corner.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 33").

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figure text 5.5–6.9 pt** in all five figures (pp. 6, 8, 12, 14, 21).
- **In-plot labels placed on data** (Fig 39.2; compare Figs 37.2 and 38.1). A generic rule would help: labels need a white halo or must sit in empty space.
- **Unsplittable code blocks and figures** cause the blanks (pp. 4, 10, 20) and the orphaned lines (p. 20).
- **Callouts:** "Watch out" (peach, pp. 10, 15, 21), "When calibration matters" and "Fairness vocabulary" (light blue, pp. 13, 23) are consistent.
- **Output blocks** all fit their width in this chapter (no column-0 wraps), unlike Ch38.
- **Folios** "n / 33", offset −1.
