# Visual review — Ch41-NLP-Foundations.pdf (33 pages)
Verdict: A clean, code-heavy chapter. Code, output and callouts are set consistently and there are no tables in the body apart from the mistakes table. Both figures have problems. In Figure 41.2 (word2vec PCA) two labels print on top of each other, and the colour groups are never explained. Figure 41.1 is set at 6–7 pt, and its caption promises NMF topics that the figure does not show. Two answer numbers are stranded at page feet, and two pages are one-third to one-half blank.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio ("n / 32") is PDF page − 1.

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body; DejaVu Sans Mono 7.9 pt code; DejaVu Sans 8.3 pt tables; Poppins headings. No fallback fonts (there is no ₹ in this chapter; amounts print as "Rs"). No tofu.
- **Images:** none; both figures are vector.
- **Figure text size** (pdfplumber char sizes): Fig 41.1 5.9–7.2 pt (heat-map labels 5.9–6.4, word lists 6.7); Fig 41.2 6.9–7.4 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** none.
- **Blank-area scan** (last ink as a share of page height): p. 2 45% (contents), p. 4 57%, p. 19 66%, p. 33 30% (chapter end).
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets. Both figures, the mistakes table and all answer pages were zoomed at 150–250 dpi.
