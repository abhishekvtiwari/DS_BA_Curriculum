# Visual review — Ch48-Big-Data-and-Distributed-Compute.pdf (24 pages)
Verdict: A strong chapter with clear plans and outputs and well-used tables. Its two figures carry the key ideas (partitions/shuffle, and reading a plan) but are set at 5.2–6.2 pt. In Figure 48.1 the red shuffle curves are drawn through the "shuffle: rows move" labels. Printed query plans are cut off at the right edge ("…output=[machine_i", "…,fal") with no ellipsis. Page flow has a 40% blank page before Figure 48.1, a 41% blank page after the 48.6 question, a section heading stranded with one bullet, and one table row carried onto the next page.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio ("n / 23") is PDF page − 1.

- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body; DejaVu Sans Mono 7.9 pt code; DejaVu Sans 8.3 pt tables. No DejaVu Serif fallback: "°C" and "₹" render in Lora here, and amounts in output are "Rs". The Polars table's box-drawing characters (p. 13) render as dashed verticals, which is legible. No tofu.
- **Images:** none; the figures are vector.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 48.1 (p. 6) 5.2–6.2 pt (partition labels 5.4); Fig 48.2 (p. 9) plan text 5.5 pt mono, notes 5.7–5.9 pt.

- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets. Both figures, all tables, every plan block and the Polars/DuckDB/Spark outputs were zoomed at 150–200 dpi.
