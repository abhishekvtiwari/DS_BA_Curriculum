# Visual review — Ch22-Statistics-Without-Fooling-Yourself.pdf (26 pages)
Verdict: Clear charts and tables overall. The figures carry the argument, and three of them have label collisions: the true-mean label runs over the intervals in 22.1, the title touches the "23.7%" value label in 22.3, and a legend sits on a bar in 22.4. Figure 22.4's legend and bar values are also only about 4.7 pt, and those values are the numbers of the Simpson's-paradox story. Pagination leaves a 31% gap on p. 4, strands the section 22.9 heading and lead-in at a page foot, and orphans a single table row onto p. 9. Code blocks in the A/B test section wrap heavily to column 0.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 25").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Chart text is outlined, so it was measured from pixels at 600 dpi. "₹", "≈", "α" and arrows fall back to DejaVu Serif in body text (pp. 3, 6, 9, 11, 17, 21–23, 25). Subscripts (H₀, H₁) and superscripts (10⁻⁸, 0.95²⁰) render. No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster. Figures 22.1–22.4 are vector.
- **Figure text size:** tick labels about 6.3–6.6 pt (Fig 22.1–22.4). Fig 22.4 legends ("BlueCart", "Upcountry") and bar value labels ("95.3", "94.1") are about 4.5–4.7 pt. The bold titles are about 7 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** exercise numbers "10."–"21." at x = 48 pt, first digit clipped (pp. 22–23).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 4 31% empty, p. 16 about 15%. p. 26 is the chapter end.
- **Code-wrap scan:** column-0 continuations on pp. 6, 7 (×6), 15 (×2), 16 (×2).
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets, and all four figures, the tables and the code blocks were zoomed at 200–600 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Chart label collisions** (pp. 5, 10, 14). Annotations are placed at fixed data coordinates without checking against titles, lines or bars.
- **Column-0 code wraps** are concentrated in the A/B section (pp. 6–7) and the survey/regression examples (pp. 15–16).
- **Page-foot strandings:** pp. 4, 8→9, 16. A keep-with-next rule for headings and "…:" lead-ins, plus keeping short tables together, fixes all three.
- **Tables** (pp. 8–9, 17–18) are consistent. The "What a p-value is not" table is clear.
- **Colour:** Fig 22.3 uses green/orange/red for 1/5/20 checks. The bars are also labelled with values and x categories, so colour is not the only cue.
- **Callouts:** Watch out (pp. 6, 16) and a write-up template (p. 17, blue). They are consistent.
- **Folios** "n / 25". The running footer is correct.
