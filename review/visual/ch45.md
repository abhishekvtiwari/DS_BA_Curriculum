# Visual review — Ch45-Data-Ingestion-and-Integration.pdf (34 pages)
Verdict: A well-structured chapter with four useful figures, but all four set their text at 5.2–7.1 pt, below the print minimum. Two of them (45.2 and 45.3) carry their meaning in red versus green. The code is the most wrap-prone in this batch: long SQL-in-Python lines wrap back to column 0 on about ten pages. Two pages are a third blank because a figure moves whole to the next page. Callout styling (Watch out, Simplification, Tool, Dialect, Real-life, Interview extra point) is consistent.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio ("n / 33") is PDF page − 1.

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body; DejaVu Sans Mono 7.9 pt code; DejaVu Sans 8.3 pt tables. DejaVu Sans-Oblique is used for an italic "and" inside a table cell (p. 24), which is correct. No ₹ in this chapter. No tofu.
- **Images:** none; the figures are vector.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 45.1 (p. 5) 5.2–7.1 pt, mostly 5.7; Fig 45.2 (p. 10) 5.8–6.5 pt, with the grey footnote line at 5.8; Fig 45.3 (p. 14) 6.0–6.2 pt; Fig 45.4 (p. 19) 5.5–6.0 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** none. Exercise numbers 10.–14. hang slightly left (p. 30).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 2 53% (contents), p. 4 64%, p. 9 63%, p. 17 79%, p. 21 74%, p. 34 76% (chapter end).
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets, and every figure, table and long code block was zoomed at 150–250 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figure text 5.2–6.5 pt** on all four figures (pp. 5, 10, 14, 19). This is the same root cause as the pilot: the figures are drawn wide and scaled down.
- **Red/green as the only signal** in Figures 45.2 and 45.3.
- **Code wraps to column 0**: about 13 pages (see V45.7).
- **Figure pushed to the next page** leaving one-third blank: pp. 4, 9.
- **Tables** (pp. 5, 6, 16, 21, 24, 25–26) share a consistent blue header; Figure 45.3 is the odd one out.
- **Callouts:** Watch out (peach) pp. 10, 13, 16, 17, 23, 25; blue notes pp. 6, 7, 14, 19, 20, 21, 24. Consistent.
- **Folios** "n / 33", chapter-local; the footer is correct throughout.
