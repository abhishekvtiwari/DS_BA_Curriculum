# Visual review — Ch30-Inference-and-Experiments.pdf (37 pages)

Verdict: A clean, well-paced chapter. The code, output and callouts are set consistently, and the experiment-plan table is easy to scan. The sample-size formula on p. 14 is the worst problem: the combining macron for p̄ lands on the parentheses, so the printed formula shows "p(1 − p)" with marks on the brackets. All four figures are drawn too wide and scaled down to 4.5–5.5 pt text. Two figures are out of numerical order, and the Figure 30.4 caption refers to "the line", which the figure doesn't draw.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 36").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body (Type 3); DejaVu Sans tables and figures; DejaVu Sans Mono code; Poppins headings. Fallbacks: "₹" in DejaVu Serif (pp. 8, 16); α and β in DejaVu Serif Italic inside the p. 14 formula. No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster (all figures are vector).
- **Figure text size:** Fig 30.1 (p. 6) 4.5–4.9 pt; Fig 30.2 (p. 15) 4.9–5.3 pt; Fig 30.4 (p. 17) 5.3–5.5 pt; Fig 30.3 (p. 21) 5.3–5.7 pt. All are below the 7 pt minimum.
- **Text outside margins:** none flagged. **Sparse pages:** p. 2 (contents) only by prescan. A pixel scan finds p. 7 20% and p. 24 16% blank, and p. 37 (chapter end) 66%.
- **Hyphen/wrap scan:** "two-|sided" inside a string in the code on pp. 15–16. The prose hyphens "multiple-testing" (p. 14) and "one-page" (p. 27) are correct compounds.
- Every page was viewed on 6-up sheets, and every figure, formula and wrapped code line was zoomed at 200–600 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figures scaled down** (pp. 6, 15, 17, 21): same root cause as the pilot. The figures are drawn about 1.5–2× too wide, so the text lands at 4.5–5.7 pt.
- **Answer numbers or lead-ins stranded at page foot:** pp. 24, 35. No page is worse than 20% blank before the chapter end.
- **Code-wrap without continuation marker:** pp. 5, 15, 16, 22, 33, 35.
- **Callouts:** Watch out (orange), Simplification note, Try it and Tool note (blue) are consistent with the rest of the book.
- **Tables:** the Common-mistakes table splits pp. 25→26 with its header repeated (fine). The numeric "Rows" column on p. 4 is right-aligned (good).
- **Folios:** "n / 36", chapter-local. The running footer is correct on every page.
