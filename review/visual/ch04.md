# Visual review — Ch04-Numbers-Without-Fear.pdf (25 pages)
Verdict: A number-dense chapter that is mostly well set: formula boxes are distinct and tables are tidy. The four charts are the weak point. Their labels run 4.8–6.6 pt, and Figure 4.2's caption relies on "red" and "green" to tell the lines apart. The main layout faults are three short tables split with a single row stranded, a lead-in stranded before its formula, and a 30% gap before Figure 4.2.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio ("n / 24") is PDF page − 1.

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. "₹" falls back to DejaVu Serif / Serif-Bold / Serif-Italic on 19 of 25 pages (the most of any chapter in this batch), including bold totals and italic quotes. The minus sign (U+2212) renders in Lora. No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 4.1 5.5–6.6 pt; Fig 4.2 5.1–5.7 pt; Fig 4.3 5.7–6.5 pt; Fig 4.4 4.8–5.7 pt (axis ticks 4.8 pt).
- **Text outside margins:** exercise numbers 10–15 at x < 50 pt (pp. 22, 23).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 8 30%, p. 10 18%, p. 25 61% (chapter end).
- Every page was viewed at 70 dpi. All charts, formula boxes, the Spreadsheet link boxes and all tables were zoomed at 150–200 dpi.
