## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figure text below print minimum** on pp. 6, 8, 10, 14 and 16. The figures appear to be drawn wide (about 1000 px) and scaled into the 150 mm column. Fix once in the figure template: draw at column width with a 7 pt minimum.
- **Triple-titled figures** on pp. 6, 8, 10, 14 and 16. Each has an in-figure bold title, an italic subtitle and a separate italic caption. That wastes space and sometimes repeats text (V25.14). Keep either the caption or the in-figure title.
- **One light-blue "callout" style** covers Chapter-at-a-glance, requirements (BR/FR/NFR), use cases, user stories, acceptance criteria and model answers (pp. 3, 11–12, 14–16, 20, 27–28). Readers cannot tell a worked specimen from a reader aid. Consider a distinct "specimen" style, such as a white box with a grey rule and a monospace ID column. No Watch out / Try it / Simplification note / Interview extra point callouts appear in this chapter.
- **Tables:** consistent blue header, repeated header on page breaks (p. 21 continuation is correct), DejaVu Sans 8.3 pt. The first column is bold in some tables (pp. 9, 12, 16 Lane table) and regular in others (pp. 5, 15, 20, 22). Pick one.
- **Straight quotes** ("we need more space", 'SHARMA HW JAN') throughout the body, where Lora has typographic quotes. Low.
- **Folio format** "n / 28" is web-style and restarts in each chapter. Contents is page 1 and the cover is unnumbered. Decide book-level folios before print.
