# Visual review — Ch08-The-Career-Tree-How-Skills-Unlock-Roles.pdf (28 pages)
Verdict: A long chapter with four figures, a skills-matrix table and several data tables, generally well set. The figures are 4.5–5.7 pt, and the heatmap (Figure 8.2) repeats the table on the facing page in a weaker form. Page flow is the main weakness. Two consecutive pages end 27–32% empty around the job description, the 8.8 heading sits alone above a 30% gap, and "Stretch goals" is stranded.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio ("n / 27") is PDF page − 1.
