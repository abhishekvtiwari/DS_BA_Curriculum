# Visual review — Ch40-Time-Series-and-Forecasting.pdf (37 pages)

Verdict: Strong content presentation. The SARIMA summary, backtest tables and plan table are all well set in monospace, and the figures are well chosen. Two problems stand out. The ACF/PACF figure contradicts its caption: it shows red "significant" bars at lags 9 and 22, not only lag 12. Two pages are about half empty (pp. 19, 23) because long code blocks never split. All four figures are at 5.3–6.1 pt, and several lead-ins and one section heading are stranded at page feet.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**. The printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 36").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora (Type 3) body; Poppins headings; DejaVu Sans tables and figures; DejaVu Sans Mono code. DejaVu Serif / Serif-Bold fallback on pp. 10–12, 28, 29, 31, 32 for α β γ, ₁₂, ₛ, ±. In "(P, D, Q)ₛ" and "(0, 1, 1)₁₂" the subscripts come from the fallback and are visibly lighter than Lora. No tofu.
- **Images:** none raster.
- **Figure text size:** Fig 40.1 5.4–5.6 pt; Fig 40.2 5.4–6.1 pt; Fig 40.3 5.3–6.0 pt; Fig 40.4 5.4–5.9 pt.
- **Text outside margins:** two-digit list numbers at x ≈ 47.7 pt (p. 30).
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 19 0.39, p. 23 0.44, p. 4 0.57, p. 10 0.60, p. 35 0.62, p. 24 0.64, p. 14 0.68, p. 8 0.71, p. 33 0.73, p. 37 0.49 (end).
