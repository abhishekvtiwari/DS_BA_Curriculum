# Visual review — Ch31-Causal-Inference-Without-Experiments.pdf (32 pages)

Verdict: A well-structured chapter. Its code, output and tables are consistent, and the method-comparison table (p. 18) is very readable. The five figures are the weak point, as elsewhere in the book: text is 4.8–5.5 pt. The balance chart (Fig 31.2) also relies on red versus green dots and has no x-axis scale. There is one large blank area (p. 13) and one stranded answer number (p. 28).

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 31").

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figures scaled down** (pp. 9, 12, 14, 16, 18). The text is always about 5 pt: the figures are drawn about 1.6× too wide for the text block.
- **Colour as the only channel:** red and green before/after (p. 12), and colour-coded strength boxes (p. 18). In greyscale, Fig 31.2's two dot colours become near-identical mid-greys.
- **Stranded answer numbers:** p. 28. **Big blank areas:** p. 13.
- **Tables:** the Common-mistakes table (p. 20) and the method table (p. 18) are clean, with no numeric columns. The p. 5 "What happened / True effect / Method" table puts bold numbers inside text, which is fine.
- **Callouts:** Watch out (pp. 8, 10, 12, 15, 17), Interview extra point (p. 19). They are consistent.
- **Folios** "n / 31"; the running footer is correct throughout.
