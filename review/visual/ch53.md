# Visual review — Ch53-Deep-Learning-in-Depth.pdf (36 pages)
Verdict: The longest chapter in the batch, and generally well built. The worked numbers, output blocks and tables are clear, and the six raster images of Figure 53.1 are adequate for pixel-art content. The main faults are figures numbered out of order (53.2 appears before 53.1, and 53.4 before 53.3) and a label in Figure 53.4 that overlaps the plotted line. Four answer numbers are stranded at page feet, and the cover eyebrow runs into the decorative rule.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 35").

Automated checks:
- **Fonts:** all embedded. Lora 10.4 pt body; DejaVu Sans Mono 7.9 pt code. DejaVu Serif / Serif-Italic / Serif-Bold appear as fallbacks for "₹" (pp. 15–16, 23, 34) and for the subscript digits in the p. 4 formula. No tofu.
- **Images (pdfimages -list):** 6 raster images, all on p. 14 (Figure 53.1). Each is 200 × 200 px RGB (ICC), about 2.6 KB, placed at **105 ppi** (about 48 × 48 mm). They are 32 × 32 source images upscaled 6.25× (non-integer), so the pixel blocks are 6 or 7 px wide. At 200 dpi the blocks look crisp and even, and the content is meant to look pixelated. They are below the 300 ppi print norm, but no blur is visible (V53.10).
- **Figure text size:** Fig 53.2 (p. 8) 5.2–6.6 pt; Fig 53.4 (p. 17) 5.2–6.6 pt; Fig 53.3 (p. 20) 5.7–6.6 pt; Fig 53.1 (p. 14) text is 7.5 pt or larger.
- **Figure order (caption scan):** 53.2 p. 8 → 53.1 p. 14 → 53.4 p. 17 → 53.3 p. 20.
- **Blank-area scan:** p. 2 49%; otherwise no half-empty pages.
- Every page was viewed on contact sheets. All figures, tables, and code/output blocks were zoomed at 150–300 dpi.

## Patterns (things that repeat on many pages — describe once, list pages)
- **Figure numbering follows source order, not page order** (also in Ch54).
- **Answer numbers stranded at page foot** (pp. 29, 30, 32, 33). A global keep-with-next rule for bold "N." answer labels fixes these.
- **Figure text below 7 pt** (pp. 8, 17, 20).
- **Two-digit list numbers clipped** (p. 28).
- **Tables:** consistent; the cost table on p. 15 is an output block, right-aligned by the code (good).
- **"₹"** renders via the DejaVu Serif fallback; barely visible.
- **Folios** "n / 35", correct.
