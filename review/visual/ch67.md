# Visual review — Ch67-The-Architect-as-Leader.pdf (15 pages)
Verdict: This is a clean, prose-led chapter with good typography and consistent callouts. The three figures repeat the Part VII defect: text at 4.4–5.7 pt inside mostly empty boxes. The timeline in Figure 67.3 runs through its own chapter labels. The cover and closing page call this "the final chapter of the book", yet Part VIII (Ch 68 onward) follows. The board-pitch quote uses Indian digit grouping (₹2,03,775) where Ch 65–66 print the same figures as ₹203,775.

Page numbers below are **PDF pages**; the printed folio is PDF page − 1 ("n / 14").
