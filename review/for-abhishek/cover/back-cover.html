Who it is for

Anyone preparing for a data science, analytics or business-analyst interview in India: analyst, data scientist, data engineer, analytics engineer or BA, from a first job to a senior move. With take-home assignments and mock interviews to run end to end.

What it does not do

It makes no promise about salaries, placements or outcomes, and it names no employer. It is a question bank and a method. The work is still yours.
