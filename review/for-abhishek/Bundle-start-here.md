# Start here

*The Data Science & Analytics Interview Book, by Compounza. Bundle arranged 3 October 2026.*

This bundle is arranged the way the product is sold: the interview book first, then the two
add-ons a reader can buy on top of it. Open the folders in number order.

| Folder | What it is | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **01 · The Interview Book** | *Be Interview Ready*: 1,160 data science and analytics interview questions with worked answers, plus the front cover, back cover and Instagram image | Everyone. This is the product |
| **02 · Add-on: The Volumes** | The three volumes (Theory, Practical, Implementation), the practice files arranged by tool, and the topic guide | Readers who want to learn the material properly, not just rehearse it |
| **03 · Add-on: Projects** | The project catalogue: ready-made portfolio projects with real, messy data and an interview story | Sold one at a time, separately |
| **04 · For review only** | What changed, the review state, the selling strategy and the internal overview | Not for buyers |

## 01 · The Interview Book

*Be Interview Ready* is the front of everything. Sixteen question banks, 1,160 questions, each one
with a one-line answer, a three-tier model answer, the likely follow-ups, the red flag, and where
in the volumes the idea is taught. It stands on its own: a reader who buys nothing else still gets
a complete book.

## 02 · Add-on: The Volumes

The volumes are where the interview book's "Learn it in" pointers lead. Buying them turns the
interview book from a rehearsal into a course.

- **Volume 1, Theory** (Parts 0 and 1). What data is, how a business runs on it, numbers without
  fear, thinking like an analyst, and the map of data jobs and how skills unlock them. No software
  needed.
- **Volume 2, Practical** (Parts 2 and 3). Spreadsheets, SQL, data cleaning, charts, Power BI,
  Python, statistics and business skills, then advanced SQL and data modelling, dbt, experiments
  and causal inference.
- **Volume 3, Implementation** (Parts 4 to 7). Machine learning and data science, data
  engineering, production ML, generative AI and MLOps, and architecture, governance and leadership.
- **Practice files.** Every dataset, workbook, query and script the volumes use, arranged by tool
  (Excel, SQL, Python and so on) rather than by chapter, each running from the smallest idea to a
  finished thing you can run.
- **The topic guide.** Every chapter, section and term, so any topic can be found in seconds.

## 03 · Add-on: Projects

Ready-made portfolio projects, bought one at a time. Each is meant to carry a realistic brief,
genuinely messy data, a worked and runnable solution, the reasoning behind it, and the part
nobody else sells: how to talk about the project in an interview.

**None is built yet.** This folder holds the catalogue and the design, so the shape of the offer
is visible. The first project to build is the daily report that sends itself.
