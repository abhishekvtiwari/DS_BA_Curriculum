## 01 · The Interview Readiness Book

*Be Interview Ready* is the front of everything: seventeen question banks and 1,226 questions,
covering the whole journey from the online screening test to the first 90 days in the new job.
Each question has a one-line answer, a three-tier model answer, the likely follow-ups, the red flag,
and where in the volumes the idea is taught. It stands on its own: a reader who buys nothing else
still gets a complete book.

**New in this version: Chapter 68A, "The Rounds Nobody Prepares For"** (66 questions) — the timed
online test and its hidden test cases, campus drives and the group discussion, live coding and
virtual rounds, messaging recruiters, following up after an interview, handling silence and
rejection, reading the offer letter, and the first 90 days.

## 02 · Add-on: The Three Volumes

The volumes are where the interview book's "Learn it in" pointers lead. Buying them turns the
interview book from a rehearsal into a course. The names follow the series title, *Analyst to
Architect*:

- **Volume 1, First Principles** (Parts 0 and 1). What data is, how a business runs on it, numbers
  without fear, thinking like an analyst, and the map of data jobs and how skills unlock them. No
  software needed.
- **Volume 2, The Working Analyst** (Parts 2 and 3). Spreadsheets, SQL, data cleaning, charts,
  Power BI, Python, statistics and business skills, then advanced SQL and data modelling, dbt,
  experiments and causal inference.
- **Volume 3, Builder to Architect** (Parts 4 to 7). Machine learning and data science, data
  engineering, production ML, generative AI and MLOps, and architecture, governance and leadership.
- **Practice files.** Every dataset, workbook, query and script the volumes use, arranged by tool
  (Excel, SQL, Python and so on) rather than by chapter.
- **The topic guide.** Every chapter, section and term, so any topic can be found in seconds.

## 03 · Add-on: Projects

Eleven ready-made portfolio projects in four tracks — analyst, business analyst, data science, and
engineering and AI — each with a realistic brief, messy data, a runnable worked solution, the
reasoning, and how to talk about it in an interview. Every one is built on material the book
already contains. **None is built yet**; this folder holds the catalogue. The first to build is P1,
the daily report that sends itself.
