# Ch 53 — Deep Learning in Depth
Verdict: A strong, honest chapter. It does one full training step by hand (forward pass, backward pass, update, all checkable), computes a convolution by hand, and works attention on four tokens. The defect project teaches "accuracy lies on imbalanced data" and "the threshold is a money decision" better than most textbooks. For a first-time reader, though, it has wrong numbers in the explanations (§53.1 and §53.2 prose, Figure 53.4's "Accuracy is 92% at every row", which is false: it ranges from 99.2% to 86.1%). The chain rule is used but never taught. Five of the seven "techniques that make training work" (Adam, dropout, batch norm, initialization, schedules) get only a table row, with no small worked example. Chapter 43, which the reader has just done, is never mentioned. The defect data is used in §53.3 before it's introduced or generated, and the threshold is tuned on the test set, which contradicts the chapter's own Common-mistakes table.
Size: ~1,430 lines / 34 pages (4–37 of Part VI). Time needed stated: 14–18 hours over three weeks; plausible? Yes for the current text (27 pages of teaching, 18 exercises, an 8-step project, feature extraction taking about a minute). With the by-hand additions proposed below (chain rule, softmax row, pooling, Adam/dropout/batch-norm mini-examples, the data-setup step), 17–20 hours is more honest.

## What works (keep)
- §53.2 is exactly the book's principle: a 2-2-1 network with every number printed, the backward pass written out one gradient per line, and a one-step update that visibly lowers the loss (1.420 → 1.019). Figure 53.2 summarises it well. Every number recomputes exactly, and a numerical nudge confirms dW2[0] = 1.764. Answer 3's learning-rate sweep (0.05 / 0.5 / 1.5) is a great divergence demo, and I verified it.
- §53.5 convolution by hand (Sobel kernel on a 5×5 square, one window computed explicitly) and Answer 5's horizontal counterpart are clear and correct.
- §53.6's threshold-by-cost table and the sentence "A data scientist who reports '92% accuracy' has said nothing" teach operating points in business terms. All eight cost rows, recalls and precisions recompute exactly. The night-shift story and Exercise 10 (brightness robustness) connect method to failure in the field.
- §53.7 attention on four tokens reproduces to three decimals, and the "Queries and keys decide who is listened to. Values decide what is heard" line (plus Answer 13) is memorable. §53.9 quantization is built from zero (scale = max/127, round, dequantize) and checked by decisions flipped rather than mean error. That's exactly the right lesson.
