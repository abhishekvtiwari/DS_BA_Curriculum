# Ch 77 — Data Engineering & Data System Design Bank
Verdict: A well-chosen, well-structured bank: the questions are ones Data Engineer interviews really ask, the model answers are mostly technically sound, and the three design walk-throughs are strong. The main problems are the "Learn it in" pointers and one untaught technique. The header admits the pointers were written without Part V's text, so most say only "Part V (this book)". Several of the precise ones are wrong: SCD points to Ch 16, topological sort to Ch 72A, §70.9 doesn't exist, and Q71-023/Q71-053 are the wrong questions. Topological sort (Kahn's algorithm) is the method Q77-018 grades against, but the book never teaches it and the chapter doesn't show the code. Two SQL claims marked "verified" only work with setup the chapter doesn't show: the upsert fails without a unique constraint, and the SCD example contradicts Ch 28's surrogate-key and half-open-date conventions.
Size: ~846 lines, ~15 pages (pp. 198–212), 35 questions (11 core/design, 24 rapid-fire). Time needed stated: none; plausible? Not stated, so can't judge. Banks are revision material, but a one-line "Time needed: ~3 hours first pass (35 questions); 45 min for the 11-item final-week list" would fit the rest of the book.

## What works (keep)
- The questions come in a sensible order: fundamentals, then basic-but-tricky, modeling, orchestration, scale, quality, and full designs. The three design cases (Q77-033 batch, Q77-034 streaming, Q77-035 dedup) explicitly reuse earlier answers, so the chapter reads as one system rather than trivia.
- The model answers are technically sound on the points that matter most. Idempotency is framed as "safe retry" (Q77-007). Exactly-once is treated as at-least-once plus idempotent processing (Q77-008), which agrees with Ch 50 §50.3. Q77-002 decides batch vs. streaming by how fresh the business decision needs the data to be. Q77-035 prefers false negatives to false merges in dedup.
- Q77-023's partition-pruning EXPLAIN reproduces exactly on PostgreSQL 16 (see Numbers checked): the plan names only `events_2025_01`. That's a real, checkable proof.
- The "quiet day" story at the end teaches a real production insight: checks for data that looks wrong vs. data that is absent. Q77-028's volume check is set up earlier, so the story pays it off.
