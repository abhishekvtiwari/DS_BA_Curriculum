# Ch 67 — The Architect as Leader
Verdict: A strong, humane closing chapter for Part VII. Its three worked scenarios (influencing without authority, saying no, presenting to a board) are concrete, and the first-90-days plan is practical. The problems are mostly about where it sits in the book and whether its references are right. It calls itself "the last chapter of the book" when Part VIII (Ch 68–82) and the Closing (Ch 83) follow. It contains an AI-authoring leftover ("verify two facts by live search … since its training data"). It sends the reader to "section 67.6" for patterns in §67.4, puts Chapter 1 in "Part I" as "one query", and inherits Chapter 66's disputed 45% figure and timeline. No code.
Size: ~541 lines / 13 pages (pp. 137–149). Time needed stated: "about two hours" to read; "years" to live. Plausible? Yes for reading. The project is correctly framed as open-ended.

## What works (keep)
- §67.4's two scripted scenarios. "Influencing without authority" starts from the other person's cost, and "Saying no" offers the version of yes that avoids the cost. Both show the failing first attempt and then the working one, which is rare and valuable.
- §67.3's five-minute board speech: it leads with the defensible floor, states the gap before anyone asks, and ties directly to Ch 66's method.
- §67.6's 90-day plan (listen, diagnose with the book's own tools, earn credibility with one small fix) and Figure 67.2's "notice what's missing: a grand redesign".
- The honest framing that judgment can't be taught by a book, and that humility is its permanent companion (§67.5).
