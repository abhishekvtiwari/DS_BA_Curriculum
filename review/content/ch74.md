# Ch 74 — Machine Learning Question Bank
Verdict: The best-aligned bank in this batch. Most "Learn it in" pointers name an exact Part IV section, and most quoted numbers match (0.823/0.978 leak, 0.845 target-encoding leak, 0.82 vs 0.78 pipeline, 0.765 → 0.633 k-NN, 1.000/0.557 tree, 0.806 NB AUC with 94% vs 27%, log loss 0.483 → 0.202, ₹1.08M vs ₹2.45M from 627 leads, MAE ₹92,933 / RMSE ₹232,567, 5.71% vs 8.06%, 30 → 44.5%). But the intro's boast that "every Learn it in reference below is precise" isn't true. Q74-036 (and Q74-001's Extra point) quote **churn-data** results as **lead data** and invert Ch 37's actual conclusion: engineered logistic regression beat boosting on churn, and boosting didn't win on leads. Q74-016 repeats a false claim that 93.5% accuracy is "worse" than 93.4%. Q74-032 and Q74-030 point to the wrong chapters. Several topics are untaught (macro/weighted F1, adjusted R², frequency/hash encoding, training-serving skew). The three "Verified, live" simulations (Q74-001, 002, 019) use unnamed data and can't be reproduced. The intro also carries a drafting leftover about "to be confirmed pointers like several other banks".
Size: ~780 extracted lines, 14 pages (pp. 143–156), 36 questions (10 core + 26 rapid-fire/walk-through). Time needed stated: none. Plausible? No estimate to judge. Suggest 6–8 hours for a reader who has done Ch 35–43.

## What works (keep)
- Numbers come from the reader's own Part IV work (Riverstone leads and churn), so every interview answer is backed by "I did this in Chapter 36/37/39". Q74-013 (confusion matrix at threshold 0.1: 100/386/46/1693 → precision 0.206, recall 0.685, F1 0.317) is exact.
- Q74-020 teaches an honest, non-obvious point: the random split scored *lower* (0.791 vs 0.823) and that isn't leakage but a lead-mix shift. It matches Ch 36 §36.9 word for word in spirit.
- Q74-002's "perfect training AUC on pure noise" demonstration is sound. My independent re-run with unregularized logistic regression (20 repeats) gives train AUC 0.59 / 0.85 / 1.00 and test ≈ 0.48–0.50 for 5 / 50 / 200 features.
- Priyanka's story (days_since_status_change as the leak) models the "name the column and say why" move well.
