# Ch 23 — Business Acumen, KPIs & Metrics
Verdict: A valuable chapter, and rare in analyst books: it connects the three financial statements, the functional metrics, the KPI tree and Goodhart's law with one company's numbers. Nearly every ratio I recomputed is exact (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, ROA/ROE, retention, LTV, the May→June bridge). For a first-time reader, the headline operating-cycle numbers (DIO 55, DSO 42, DPO 38) are asserted without any formula, though they can be derived exactly from the chapter's own balance sheet. Accounting vocabulary (depreciation, accrual, PPE, capex, leverage) is used before it is defined, despite "Nothing here assumes an accounting or finance background". Several consistency errors need fixing: the chapter calls the invented Ch 21 delivery data "real", calls Riverstone "a distributor" although Ch 3 gives it two plants, the KPI identity is claimed "exact" while the output is ₹31.89 short, and the new/retained customer counts disagree (767 vs 772; 3,827 vs 3,832).
Size: ~1,020 lines, 19 book pages (394–412), 12 code blocks (11 Python, 1 SQL), 27 exercises + timed challenge. Time needed stated: 15–18 hours over two weeks. Plausible? Yes for a reader with some business exposure. For a true beginner meeting three financial statements and about 35 metrics for the first time, it is at the upper edge. With the formula and vocabulary additions below, 17–20 hours is more honest (23.30).

## What works (keep)
- The plain-English shopkeeper analogy (the P&L is a video, the balance sheet a photograph, cash flow "the one that keeps shopkeepers awake") and the "profitable but can't make payroll" real-world story. Both are memorable and correct in principle.
- The chain-linked May→June revenue bridge (§23.11). The code is right, the three effects sum exactly to −₹35,277,085 (recomputed), and the five-step "diagnosing a KPI move" method is excellent.
- The labelling of real vs invented figures ("Each table says which is which"), the naive-vs-capped LTV warning, the "CAC and LTV need the same population" watch-out, and the vanity-metric table with its better replacements.
- §23.13's six-part metric definition, which Ch 24 reuses well.
