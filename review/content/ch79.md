# Ch 79 — GenAI, LLM & MLOps Question Bank
Verdict: The fundamentals are clear and well chosen: tokens, temperature, hallucination as expected behaviour, RAG, reranking, agents and the evaluation mix. The chunking demo reproduces character for character, and the closing chunk-boundary story is excellent. Two claims contradict what the book itself teaches. First, the MLOps section (Q79-028/031/039) presents input drift, tested with a KS p-value, as the leading indicator of model health and the preferred retraining trigger. Ch 56 §56.8–56.9 shows with Riverstone data that "input drift is not model decay", uses the KS statistic rather than a p-value, and names business events and a schedule as the best triggers. Second, Q79-033's "illustrative" prices are per-1K rates that match real per-million prices. That makes the cost example about 1,000× too high and far outside Ch 54's own "$0.05 to $50 per million" range. The pointers were written "without their approved text", so several are wrong: §35.1, prompt injection to Ch 57, circuit breaker attributed to Ch 77. Every code block is missing its imports or setup (`np`, `rng`, the text, the print loop).
Size: ~923 lines, ~16 pages (pp. 225–240), 39 questions (11 core/design, 28 rapid-fire). Time needed stated: none; plausible? Not stated. Suggest "~3 hours first pass; 45 min for the 11-item final-week list".

## What works (keep)
- Q79-007 frames hallucination as the expected behaviour of next-token prediction without grounding, not a "bug", and ties it to RAG and "I don't know" architecture. That's accurate and exactly the interview-grade framing.
- Q79-012's overlap-chunking function and output reproduce exactly (4 chunks of 60/60/60/20 characters). The story at the end (a real citation to an incomplete chunk that split "30 days" from "except electronics, 14 days") teaches a failure mode most material misses. It ends with a golden-set regression test, which ties back to Q79-023.
- Q79-013 explains reranking as a general two-stage "cheap broad pass, expensive precise pass on few" pattern. That transfers well beyond RAG.
- The "note on currency" (teach durable concepts, check current specifics) is the right stance for a GenAI bank.
