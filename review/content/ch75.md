# Ch 75 — Product Sense, Metrics, Case Studies & Guesstimates
Verdict: A useful, readable bank. The verify → external → internal → segment funnel, the "decisions first" dashboard method, the "what would have to be true" framing and the two full walk-throughs are what analyst and BA candidates need, and the guesstimate arithmetic is correct. The main problems: (1) not one "Learn it in" pointer sends the reader to a teaching chapter. They point to other question banks (69, 70, 73) or back into this chapter, while the ideas are taught in Ch 3, 4, 5, 22, 23, 24 and 30. Three pointers are broken (Ch 44's "revenue tree", §70.10, Bayes for market sizing). (2) Some model answers are wrong: RICE multiplies by effort instead of dividing, Q75-011 sends the whole investigation to acquisition, and the North Star definition contradicts Ch 23. (3) Several "Passes" rows describe the behaviour the same question then calls a red flag.
Size: ~740 lines, 14 pages (PDF pp. 157–170), 31 questions (9 core, 20 rapid-fire, 2 full walk-throughs). Time needed stated: none (no "Chapter at a glance", "Before you start" or "Time needed" block, unlike Ch 68–69); plausible? Can't judge. A realistic figure is 2.5–3.5 hours to read and drill once, plus 2–3 hours for the four-part project.

## What works (keep)
- Q75-007 and Q75-030 carry one funnel (verify the number → external → internal → segment) from a short answer through to a full live case, with a concrete confound: email-client image loading, and colder contacts added to the list lowering the average. It matches Ch 69 §69.4's worked arc and Ch 23 §23.11's "walk it down the tree".
- Q75-013 "start from the decisions" plus the explicit exclusion of a vanity metric, and Q75-031's single rate-based metric with a guardrail. Both are exactly Ch 23 §23.10–23.12's discipline, applied under interview pressure.
- Q75-019's "what would have to be true for yes", ending in a proportionate pilot, is the best product-sense framing in Part VIII.
- The Karan story shows one clarifying question changing the whole structure. It is a vivid, correct illustration of Ch 69 Move 1.
