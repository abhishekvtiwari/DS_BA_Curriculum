# Ch 76B — Business Analyst Question Bank
Verdict: Solid BA interview content. Most answers match what Ch 25 now teaches, often almost word for word (QA vs UAT, as-is before to-be, BPMN, BRD/FRD/SRS, UAT triage), and the tier and extra-point structure suits a BA reader. But the chapter was written before Ch 25 existed and still says so ("not yet written at the time of this draft… flagged for a re-check"). The re-check was never done, so the flagship answers now diverge from Ch 25. The gap-analysis model answer (Q76-028) repeats the Ch 3 invoicing contradiction and names as the root cause exactly the symptom Ch 25 §25.9 warns against automating. The swimlane says "CRM" where Ch 3/25 say ERP. "Invoice matching" means invoice-to-PO, not Ch 25's bank-line-to-invoice matching. Several Agile items are never taught (INVEST, Gherkin, spike, Definition of Done, Product Owner), and one is wrong: the Product Owner does not own the sprint backlog. The "Passes" tier of Q76-033 labels silently changing a correct number as passing.
Size: ~860 lines, 15 pages (PDF pp. 183–197), 38 questions (9 core, 28 rapid-fire, 1 full scenario). Time needed stated: none; plausible? Can't judge. Realistic: 2.5–3 hours to read and drill once, plus 4–6 hours for the five-step project (two interviews, a swimlane map, a story, a requirement).

## What works (keep)
- Q76-001's elicitation dialogue (ask for the last real instance, restate as a testable requirement) and Q76-038's full elicitation, which asks about the exception path, frequency and tolerance. Both are Ch 25 §25.3's five moves, shown live.
- Q76-029 / Q76-030–032: "QA verifies the system works as specified; UAT verifies the specification was right", who signs off, and triage on a late UAT failure. They are accurate and match Ch 25 §25.10.
- Q76-013's empty-state acceptance criterion as the unprompted edge case, and Q76-021/022's "as-is first; the as-is map is often the most valuable deliverable" (Ch 25 §25.4).
- The Farah story ("one requirement was actually three") is the right lesson and matches Ch 25 §25.3's own "haven't ordered recently" ask.
