# 3 October 2026 · Section 79.9, the predict-the-number round

The seventh bank to the 71.11 standard. Chapter 79 already covers the mechanics — tokens
(Q79-001), embeddings and cosine (Q79-002), RAG and chunking (Q79-012), reranking (Q79-013),
temperature (Q79-004), drift (Q79-028) and cost estimation (Q79-033). This section is the
arithmetic and the geometry underneath them.

**Section 79.9, "Predict the number: the arithmetic behind an AI system."** 3,740 words, 6 core
questions (Q79-040 to 045) and 10 rapid-fire rows (Q79-046 to 055), taking the chapter from 39
questions to 55. Numbered 79.9 because the chapter's last section is 79.8.
