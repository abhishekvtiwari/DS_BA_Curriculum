## Verification

Every figure came from a run on Python 3.12 with `requests` installed. The cron and backoff numbers
are arithmetic that can be checked by hand; the jitter figures use numpy with seed 78. The
`requests` and socket timeout defaults were read from the live signatures rather than from the
documentation.

Q78-033 overlaps deliberately with Chapter 77's Q77-037: the same 2⁵³ limit, reached through the
wire format rather than through a dataframe, and the question points at it rather than repeating
the explanation.
